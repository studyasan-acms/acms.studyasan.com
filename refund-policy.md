## 1. General Overview

StudyAsan provides high quality live online tutoring, educational study modules, interactive classroom management, and online test series. We believe in complete transparency and customer satisfaction.

## 2. Course & Subscription Cancellations

- **Trial & Demo Classes:** We encourage students to attend demo sessions or trial lectures prior to enrolling in paid courses.
- **Refund Window (Within 7 Days):** If a student is unsatisfied with a course, a refund request may be submitted within **7 calendar days** of the course start date, provided the student has attended fewer than 25% of the live lectures.
- **Recurring Monthly Tuition:** Students subscribed to monthly recurring batches may cancel at any time before the subsequent billing cycle begins.

---

## 3. Non-Refundable Items

- Digital downloadable items (PDF e-books, past solved question papers) once accessed or downloaded.
- One-time institutional registration or exam application fees.
- Test series or certification assessments that have already been attempted or graded.

---

## 4. Refund Processing

- Verified and approved refunds are processed back to the original source payment method (UPI, Bank Account, Net Banking, or Credit/Debit Card) within **5 to 7 business days**.
