## 1. Overview & Your Rights

In full compliance with **Google Play Store's User Data & Account Deletion Policy** and **Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1**, StudyAsan allows all registered users (students, parents, teachers, and guardians) to request complete and permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time.

---

## 2. How to Request Account & Data Deletion

### Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Recommended)
1. Open the **StudyAsan App** or web portal at [https://acms.studyasan.com](https://acms.studyasan.com).
2. Log in with your email or phone number.
3. Click your **Profile Avatar > Settings**.
4. Scroll to the **Account Security / Deletion** section.
5. Click **"Request Account Deletion"** and verify with the one-time password (OTP) sent to your registered email address.

### Method 2: Web & Email Request
If you no longer have the app installed or cannot log in:
- Send an email from your registered email address to **[contact@studyasan.com](mailto:contact@studyasan.com)**.
- **Subject:** `Account Deletion Request - [Your Name / Registered Email]`
- Please provide your registered mobile number or student/teacher ID for verification.

---

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion Scope

### What is Permanently Deleted:
* Full name, email address, phone number, and password hashes.
* Profile pictures, school name, date of birth, blood group, and residential addresses.
* Live classroom chat messages, doubt queries, homework submissions, and uploaded media attachments.
* Active class enrollments and Firebase Cloud Messaging push notification tokens.

### What May Be Temporarily Retained:
* Financial tax invoices and statutory payment receipts (retained strictly for statutory auditing and tax compliance as mandated by Indian law).
* Anonymized aggregate test statistics (stripped of any personally identifying details).

---

## 4. Deletion Timeline & Irreversibility

Once your deletion request is verified:
- Access to the platform is immediately disabled.
- All personal data is permanently purged from active and backup databases within **30 calendar days**.
- This action is permanent and cannot be reversed.
